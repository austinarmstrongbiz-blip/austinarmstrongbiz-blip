# Collage art spec

Nine images. This is the homepage, so these are the first thing anyone sees.

## How to add one

1. Make the image in Canva at the size in the table below.
2. Remove the background (Canva: select image → **Edit image** → **BG Remover**).
3. Download as **PNG**, with **transparent background** ticked.
4. Rename it to the exact filename in the table.
5. Drop it into this folder (`public/collage/`).

That is the whole job. No code to change. Any tile without a file keeps showing
its grey placeholder box, so you can add them one at a time and see each one
land.

Sizes are double what actually shows on screen. That is on purpose, so they stay
sharp on a retina laptop and on your phone.

## Rules that matter

**Do not rotate the images.** The site tilts each tile itself. If you rotate the
art too, it gets tilted twice and looks wrong. Export everything straight.

**Transparent background, not white.** A white rectangle behind the art kills
the cut-out scrapbook look, because the tiles overlap each other.

**Put the subject in the middle.** Tiles overlap at the edges, so anything near
a corner may sit underneath a neighbouring tile.

**Keep the file under about 400KB each.** Nine large images on the homepage is
the fastest way to make the site feel slow. Canva PNGs are usually fine; if one
comes out huge, run it through tinypng.com.

**A person can tell what it is in under a second.** This is navigation, not a
gallery. If someone cannot guess where a tile goes, it is the wrong image.

## What each one should be

You know the vibe better than I do. These are starting points, not rules.

- **field-notes.png** — you reading, writing, or a stack of books. Everyday, not posed.
- **essays.png** — the tall one. A portrait shot, or handwriting on paper.
- **matchday.png** — Man City. A shirt, the Etihad, a scarf, a TV mid-match.
- **projects.png** — something you built. A screen, a whiteboard, a laptop mid-work.
- **about.png** — your face. This is the anchor tile in the middle of the collage.
- **work.png** — the professional one. Suit, desk, on stage.
- **the-homies.png** — people. The only tile that should have more than one person in it.
- **cv.png** — the small wide one. A printed page, a headshot, keep it simple.
- **none-of-the-above.png** — the newsletter. A logo, a cover, or the words themselves.

## Also useful

Handwritten words photographed on paper and background-removed read as more
personal than a Canva text box, and they suit the scrapbook feel. Worth trying
on one or two tiles.
