What a weird game. City had 58% of the ball, outshot Fulham 15 to 5, and racked up 1.87 xG to their 0.74, and we still drew 1-1 at home, because only 0.46 xG of our shots actually hit the target. The first half looked absolutely abysmal, the same as it has all season, so I'm not surprised by that part anymore. What I can't get past is the one thing that actually worked. Elliot Anderson finally played higher up the pitch as an 8 instead of sitting in the 6, he was our best player by a margin that isn't close, and he scored a goal that looked like Kevin De Bruyne. So why the hell haven't we been doing this all season? This one is on Maresca.

Even against a lower, softer press, we got beaten on the counter again in the 13th minute, and nothing came of it only because Khusanov is so solid, even out of position at right back. Three minutes later we weren't so lucky. Fulham countered from a throw-in, Oscar Bobb cooked Anderson, and Rodrigo Muniz scored. Where was Dias? He's playing this ego game where he thinks he's much better than he is, and it keeps putting us in these calamity situations. He won 3 of his 11 duels. The care to defend was lackadaisical from everybody except Ndiaye, Anderson and Khusanov, and in the 30th minute another counter nearly made it 2-0. That's the frustrating part: Fulham only had 0.74 xG and 1 big chance all game. They didn't need more, because we keep handing it to them.

Then there's the Haaland goal that wasn't. Anderson switched the play, Ndiaye crossed, Haaland tapped it in, and the referee said Leno had possession. What is even that? It's the worst call I've seen all season, and VAR didn't step in. I guess if the keeper has one hand on it and it's pinned against the ground, that's possession. But Leno blatantly took his hand off the ball before Haaland touched it, so it should have been a goal. It was a bad call, and the rule needs changing too. The bigger problem is that Haaland had plenty of other chances. Foden set him up in the 35th and he skied it over the bar. Anderson broke and found him in the 42nd and he missed again. I predicted Haaland would score at least 2 today. He finished with 1.03 xG, 4 shots and 1 on target. Is that him or the service? Both, but mostly him today. His finishing was poor and he should have had more goals.

The second half is why I'm writing this. In the 57th minute Anderson hit an amazing full volley off a cross, and it looked like Kevin De Bruyne. Two minutes later Haaland flicked it on for him and he slipped, and in the 65th Haaland found him again for another great chance. That's what happens when you let him push into an 8 instead of anchoring him at the 6. FotMob made him player of the match at 8.1. He took 4 shots, created 3 chances and had 7 touches in their box. He was our best player today and it isn't close. He's been sitting in front of the back line for most of the season, and the whole time this was the version of him we needed. That means Bouaddi has to be the 6. It has to be him.

Foden played really well but wasn't influential. He won the ball back high early and set up Haaland, he had a great chance in the 52nd, and he created a lot of chances for Haaland. If Haaland converts one of them, I'm writing a different sentence about Foden. Iliman Ndiaye had a great game and should have had the assist on the disallowed goal. FotMob has him with 0.56 xA, the most on our team, with 3 chances created and 27 of 28 passes completed. I said before kickoff that he and Doku would each take their man on 5 or more times. Doku tried it 5 times and completed 2. Ndiaye tried 3 and completed 1. Doku was fine, but nothing crazy. Just an average performance.

The back line around Anderson was a mixed bag. Khusanov had a great game, and in the 82nd minute he covered about 30 yards to slide in and win the ball back for a throw. Nico O'Reilly had a poor one. He was way out of position too often. That's definitely the tactics, but it's odd that it was this bad, because he's played that way in previous seasons and we've always had cover behind him. Guéhi looked very wary again. FotMob liked him, with 86 of 90 passes and 5 tackles, but he just doesn't look sure of himself. He still looks injured to me.

Donnarumma, there he is. His save in the 67th minute was elite, and for the first time in weeks he looked locked in. FotMob has him at 0.00 goals prevented, exactly average, which after Leipzig and Forest is a win. He got a yellow for dissent in the 76th, and I'm fine with it. It came after a corner we never should have given up, because it came off them first. Bouaddi was a tale of two halves: pretty poor in the first, much better in the second. Quick hits on the subs: Cherki came on for Foden in the 70th and was very influential, winning the ball and driving forward. He just needs one less touch more consistently. Allan did what he could, nothing crazy. Enzo came on in the 85th, and I honestly don't know if he touched the ball. FotMob says 4 times.

The second half got better because Anderson went higher and the game opened up, and that only makes it more frustrating. The answer has been on the pitch the whole time. This one, in my opinion, is really on Maresca and his tactics.
